Amp & board

The amp head sits above a carpeted pedalboard. Pedals of different sizes sit a little crooked and are joined by patch cables from jack to jack.

Channel selector

Turn the six-way rotary switch or tap an engraved preset. Saved sounds are cassettes on a wooden shelf; pick one to load or share it.

Rack tuner

A rack-mount unit with ears and screws, a backlit needle meter, and flat / tune / sharp lamps.

Back panel

Settings live on the amp's rear: a model sticker, footswitch and USB sockets, a write-protect switch and silkscreened notes.
