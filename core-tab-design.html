Core Tab: design

The full app in one style, combining your reference screenshots: a graphite amp head with big numbers and orange arcs, rounded effect modules with power buttons and glowing LCDs, a preset bar with arrows, faceplate skins, and a hardware-style Stage mode. Everything is clickable: drag knobs up or down, tap power buttons, use the ◀ ▶ arrows. Features marked NEEDS V4 CAPTURE appear in the app but stay disabled until your amp's USB log confirms their commands.

Amp head

Big numbers above the knobs, orange arcs, grille, and three skins: graphite, silver, vintage gold. Voice is an LCD with arrows, since the V4 has six voices.

Effect modules

Each effect is a rounded module with a power button, an LCD and its own colour. Tapping a module's power button copies the amp: off → on → focused → off.

Knob names change

The knob labels follow the V4 manual. Phaser shows Mix / Depth / Speed; Envelope shows Sensitivity / Depth; delays show Feedback / Level / Time.

Stage mode

Hardware-style giant pads for playing live: patches, effects, tap and gain. Readable from a music stand.
